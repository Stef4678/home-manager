Home Manager

Puts your household bills, chores and meter readings inside Eagle, with real due dates and reminders. Attach your own Eagle items — a meter photo, an invoice, a receipt — and it tags them, so Eagle itself remembers which items belong to which record.

Highlights

- Attach your own Eagle items to any bill, task, meter or reading — from a searchable picker or Eagle's current selection
- Each attached item is tagged in place (hm:b3, hm:todo), so the link lives in your library and is rebuilt on every load
- Complete a task or pay a bill and its items flip to hm:done; detach and the tag comes off
- Bills with amount, due date and a repeat rule — marking one paid records it and schedules the next
- Tasks with due dates, priorities and recurrence, plus water meters that turn readings into consumption and a chart
- Reminders as Eagle notifications, a dashboard of what is due, and one gallery of every attached item

How to use

1. Open it — first run adds an electricity bill, a gas bill, two water meters and two tasks.
2. Press + From Eagle on any record to attach items, or use your current Eagle selection.
3. Complete a task or pay a bill and the attached items flip to hm:done in Eagle.

Install

Needs Eagle 4.x. No dependencies and no build step, so double-click the .eagleplugin or copy the plugin folder into Eagle's plugin folder. Reminders run while the plugin window is open, and items in other libraries show a padlock until you switch to that library and sync. No network requests — it writes only its own data file and the hm: tags on the items you attach.
